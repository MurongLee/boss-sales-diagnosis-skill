# Diagnostic Question Bank

Ask only questions that can materially change the diagnosis. If the user gave enough context, proceed and label assumptions.

## Price Objection

Use when the customer says the price is high, asks for discount, or compares a cheaper competitor.

High-value questions:
- 客户说贵，是在需求确认前、方案前、报价后，还是竞品介入后说的？
- 你现在接触的是使用人、采购、部门负责人，还是最终拍板人？
- 客户认为贵，是总价贵、单价贵、预算不够，还是觉得你的差异不值这个价？

Judgment shift:
- If price objection appears before value consensus, diagnose premature quotation.
- If after proposal with no next step, diagnose value translation and decision-chain weakness.
- If procurement says it, diagnose negotiation role; if business owner says it, diagnose value/risk mismatch.

## Proposal Silence

Use when the proposal, quotation, demo summary, or contract draft has been sent and the customer goes quiet.

High-value questions:
- 发方案前有没有约定下一步，比如复盘会、内部评审时间、决策人参与？
- 方案是发给联系人，还是也覆盖了决策人和影响人？
- 方案里有没有一页能让联系人直接向上解释的材料？

Judgment shift:
- No agreed next step means the seller may have mistaken sending material for advancing the deal.
- Single contact coverage means the deal may be stuck at internal forwarding.
- No executive summary means the contact received work, not help.

## Decision-Maker Access

Use when the seller cannot meet the boss, real buyer, budget owner, technical gatekeeper, or procurement controller.

High-value questions:
- 现在这个联系人为什么愿意帮你？他个人能得到什么安全感或收益？
- 他有没有能力组织下一次会议，还是只能收材料？
- 你有没有给他一个邀请决策人的正当理由？

Judgment shift:
- If the contact cannot convene others, treat them as information channel, not sponsor.
- If the contact has personal risk, first make them safer before asking for escalation.

## Low-Price Competitor

Use when a competitor offers a lower price or simpler promise.

High-value questions:
- 对方低在哪里：采购价、首付款、服务范围、交付承诺，还是关系成本？
- 客户有没有经历过低价方案带来的返工、延期、扯皮或隐性成本？
- 你的贵，是否对应客户明确在意的风险？

Judgment shift:
- If your premium maps to no customer risk, the price is probably genuinely high.
- If competitor is cheap by deleting responsibility, reframe around risk boundary.

## Relationship-Based Deal

Use when the seller says "关系很好", "认识老板", "老客户", or "一直合作".

High-value questions:
- 这次采购是否换了人、换了预算、换了标准，或进入了更正式流程？
- 关系人有没有真实决策权，还是只愿意引荐？
- 除了关系，客户是否承认你的能力差异？

Judgment shift:
- Relationship without capability trust is fragile.
- Old relationship does not automatically survive new risk, new budget, or new decision process.

## Deal Worthiness

Use for owner/operator decisions about whether to keep investing resources.

High-value questions:
- 这单金额、毛利、回款风险和交付压力大概怎样？
- 这个客户是否有复购、标杆、转介绍或战略入口价值？
- 客户现在的要求是在共同降低风险，还是只是在转移成本给你？

Judgment shift:
- Low margin plus high customization usually means Stop or Nurture.
- Strategic value can justify Repair or Invest, but only with milestones and exit conditions.
